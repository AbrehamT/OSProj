There was such a large number of minor page faults because the ROWS of the allocated
were significantly greater than the size of the process' pagetable. As a result all the pages of our process didn't get an entry in the process' page table.
Nevertheless since the size of memory was large enough and there weren't many other running processes,
the process' pages were mapped to some place in RAM but not pointed to by the process page table, i.e. a minor(soft) fault.
